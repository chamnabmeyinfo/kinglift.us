# 🎨 MOC: Brand Identity & Design System

KingLift's digital presence communicates **uncompromising strength, modern electric efficiency, and American industrial reliability**.

## Color Tokens
- **Industrial Charcoal (Background/Dark Base)**: `#0B0F19` / `#111827`
- **Safety Gold (Primary Action / Brand Accent)**: `#F59E0B` (Amber 500), `#D97706` (Amber 600)
- **High-Viz Warning / Alert**: `#EF4444` (Red 500)
- **Steel Slate (Card Borders & Subtle Gradients)**: `#1E293B` to `#334155`
- **Pure White / High-Legibility Light Text**: `#F8FAFC`

## Typography
- **Headings**: `Inter` / `Barlow Semi Condensed` / `Syne` (Bold, uppercase tracking, industrial strength).
- **Body**: `Inter` or system sans-serif (14px - 16px, high contrast).
- **Data / Spec Metrics**: `JetBrains Mono` or tabular numerals for capacities (lbs, kg, mm, in).
