# ⚡ KL-EP45Li — Pro-Lithium 4,500 lbs Electric Pallet Jack

- **48V Plug-and-Play Lithium Pack**: Recharges in under 2 hours with opportunity charging capability.
- **Pin-Code Access & Smart Tiller**: Ergonomic multi-function handle with turtle-speed creep mode for vertical-tiller maneuvering inside freight trailers.
