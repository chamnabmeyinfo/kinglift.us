# 📐 KL-ST33 — 130" Heavy Walkie Stacker

The **KL-ST33** delivers compact forklift power in a pedestrian walkie stacker footprint. Perfect for facilities without full forklift licensing requirements.

## Key Highlights
- **Adjustable Straddle Legs & Class II ITA Forks**: Accommodates both standard 48x40" GMA pallets and closed-bottom skids.
- **Dual-Stage Mast with High Visibility View Window**: Clear sightline to fork tips when lifting to high racking.
- **Regenerative Braking & Electronic Speed Limiting**: Automatic deceleration on turns.
