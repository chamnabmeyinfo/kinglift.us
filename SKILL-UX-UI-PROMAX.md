# ⚡ UX/UI ProMax Design System Specification (THIDS-PROMAX-2026)

The **UX/UI ProMax Standard** is the highest-tier design methodology for commercial heavy lifting machinery platforms. It fuses **brutal industrial strength** with **cinematic, state-of-the-art interactive micro-experiences** (comparable to Tesla Semi, Apple Pro hardware, and Porsche Engineering).

---

## 🌟 7 Core Pillars of UX/UI ProMax

### 1. Cinematic Industrial Depth & Lighting
- **Obsidian Dark Canvas (`#06090F`):** Deepest charcoal background with subtle metallic brushed titanium gradients.
- **High-Visibility Safety Gold (`#F59E0B` to `#FB8500`):** Glowing accent lighting (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`), dynamic border pulses, and active focus rings.
- **Glassmorphism with Metallic Sheen:** `backdrop-blur-xl` panels with `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)` micro-borders.

### 2. High Information Density & Engineering Authority
- B2B procurement decision makers demand immediate, accurate data.
- Never hide technical specs behind fluff. Provide exact numbers:
  - Rated Load Capacity (`lbs` and `kg`)
  - Lift Reach (`inches` and `mm`)
  - Turning Radius (`inches`)
  - Battery Chemistry (`48V LiFePO4`)
  - Structural Steel Thickness (`6mm–8mm`)

### 3. Interactive Commercial Decision Tools
- **Live Tonnage Matcher:** Dynamic visual mast elevation simulator with OSHA safety margins.
- **Side-by-Side Equipment Comparison:** 3-way spec comparison matrix with highlighted advantages.
- **Fleet TCO & ROI Calculator:** Calculates annual fuel and maintenance savings switching from Propane/Lead-Acid to KingLift Lithium.
- **Component Blueprint Inspector:** Hotspot exploration of European hydraulics, chassis welds, and lithium BMS.

### 4. Tactile Micro-Interactions & Physics
- Buttons feature micro-elevation (`transform: translateY(-2px)` on hover, `scale(0.97)` on active click).
- Interactive sliders with live numeric badges and gradient power meters.
- Non-intrusive toast notifications with titanium borders confirming RFQ actions.

### 5. Multi-Unit Imperial & Metric Native Toggling
- 1-Click global and local toggling across all spec sheets and product cards.

### 6. Dual Lighting Modes
- ☀️ **Daylight Warehouse Mode:** High-contrast crisp slate surfaces with safety gold accents for bright warehouse tablets.
- 🌙 **Heavy Midnight Mode:** Deep obsidian canvas with glowing amber highlights for command centers and desktop procurement.

### 7. Instant Zero-Friction B2B RFQ Funnel
- 2-step commercial freight checkout with dynamic operating weight calculations, receiving dock types, and instant response guarantees.
